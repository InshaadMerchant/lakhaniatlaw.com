🚀 Excited to share my first formal project for a law firm — and it's live!

I recently had the privilege of designing and building the official website for **Lakhani Law, PLLC**, a boutique Houston-based law firm specializing in Healthcare Transactions, Mergers & Acquisitions, and Business Law.

This wasn't just a website — it was a full-stack product built from scratch, production-deployed, and ready for real clients.

---

**Here's what I built:**

⚖️ **5-page professional website** — Home, About, Practice Areas, Representative Matters, and Contact — each crafted with a clean, modern law firm aesthetic (navy + gold palette, Georgia serif typography)

📅 **Custom 4-step booking system** — a fully custom consultation scheduler built without any third-party calendar libraries. Users pick a date, select a time slot, fill out their details, and receive instant confirmation

🔥 **Firebase Realtime Database integration** — every consultation booking is saved to the database in real time, giving the attorney a live view of all incoming appointments

📧 **Automated email notification system** — the moment a client books a consultation, the attorney receives an AI-formatted email notification powered by **OpenAI** and **Resend**, summarizing the booking in professional natural language

🎨 **Fully responsive UI** — built with **Material UI v5** and a custom theme, with a mobile drawer, adaptive layouts, and a sticky scroll-spy navbar that highlights the active section as you scroll

⚡ **Deployed on Vercel** with a custom domain via Squarespace DNS, environment variables secured, and optimized for production

---

**Tech Stack:**
`Next.js 14` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Material UI v5` · `Firebase Realtime Database` · `OpenAI API` · `Resend` · `Vercel` · `GitHub`
